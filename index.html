01 / WORK

AGRITECH · STARTUP OPERATIONS

GramIQ ↗

Founder's Office Intern · 2026

I got a look at startup life from a seat where no two tasks necessarily looked alike. GramIQ works in agritech, using AI to make agricultural information and support more accessible to farmers.

I researched grants and funding opportunities, including CSR-related opportunities, explored product ideas, worked on job descriptions, and helped with recruitment through resume screening and candidate conversations. I also looked into tools and processes that could help the team work better.

What stayed with me was how much of building a startup happens outside the product itself. Research, follow-ups, people, planning, and the unglamorous little details all count.
